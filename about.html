About the project

A cleaner community starts with us.

Basura Alert is a proposed community-based website that helps residents access waste collection schedules, learn proper waste segregation, and prepare for collection day.

The problem

Residents may struggle to find or remember collection times

This is the problem being investigated, not a proven result until the research is completed.

Proposed solution

What Basura Alert provides

Waste collection schedule

Centralized verified schedule.

Next-collection countdown

Live countdown to next collection.

Reminder preferences

Choose lead times.

Interactive waste guide

Searchable segregation guide.

Community concern reporting

Demonstration report form.

How it works

Four simple steps

1

Check the verified local schedule.

2

Review disposal guidance and reminders.

3

Prepare waste for the appropriate collection.

4

Use the demonstration reporting form to document a concern.

Community cooperation

Follow verified announcements

Residents should coordinate with the appropriate local office and follow verified community announcements.

Research

Community research is in progress

The planned study involves three interviews and a survey of 15 respondents. No results yet.
