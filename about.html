About Basura Alert

A cleaner community starts with us.

Basura Alert is a community-based environmental campaign that empowers residents to access waste collection schedules, learn proper waste segregation, and actively participate in building cleaner, more sustainable neighborhoods.

Get Started Report Concerns

Our Mission

What We Do

Verified Schedules

Centralized access to community waste collection schedules, ensuring residents know when to dispose of different waste types.

Smart Segregation

Comprehensive guidance on proper waste sorting into biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and special waste categories.

Community Action

Enabling residents to report concerns and work together with local authorities for a cleaner environment.

The Problem

Why Basura Alert?

Many residents struggle to find accurate, up-to-date information about waste collection schedules in their communities. This leads to improper disposal practices, missed collections, and environmental concerns.

Note: This is the problem being investigated as part of ongoing research, not a proven result until the research is completed.

Our Solution

Practical & Community-Focused

  • Easy Access
    Mobile-friendly design accessible from any device
  • Always Up to Date
    Schedule information clearly marked as verified or demo
  • Community Driven
    Encourages local participation and environmental awareness
